# P2: 12 kHz finalist verify pass on the 8 GB RTX 4060 (2026-10-23, 15:49-16:07 EDT)

`docs/NEXT_DEV_PRIORITIES.md` P2. No solver change, no cap raised, no bound touched. Everything below comes from
`designs/<hash>/metrics.json` of the verify runs (`hornlab_beat_bem` CUDA path, verified 2026-10-22).

> **Physics caveat (print with every 12 kHz number).** The source is a rigid uniform-velocity spherical cap. Above
> the throat's first cross mode (f11 = **7,914 Hz** for the 1-inch exit, computed in every record) a real
> compression driver is not a uniform cap, so **8-12 kHz results compare GEOMETRIES, not drivers**. Free-standing
> horn, rigid lossless walls, 25 log-spaced frequencies 300-12,000 Hz (isobars interpolate between them).

## How it ran

- Briefs `examples/briefs/axi_90x90_1in_12k.yaml`, `hifi_90x60_1in_12k.yaml` (copies of the shipped briefs with
  `band_hi_hz: 12000`). Verify grid 300-12,000 Hz, 25 points; verify dof cap 7,000 (config, unchanged).
- `hornlab-search verify --run <finalist run> --brief <12k brief> --top 3` (the `--brief` fix of 0cf8286), one GPU
  job at a time, `gpu-clean` before and after each (2 host processes killed after every job; none left at the end;
  VRAM 327 MiB after). Each verify writes its own run dir `runs/<ts>-verify-<id>`, listed by the GUI (isobars render
  to 12 kHz, 0 console errors).
- CPU pre-mesh first: all 12 finalist designs fit at their shipped epw (8,743-11,810 triangles). The epw+2 variants
  do not fit the verify cap (axi epw 10: 16,720-18,400 triangles > 15,784; hifi epw 8: 7,884 dofs > 7,000), so the
  convergence check compares DOWN.
- Wall time: 14 designs + 2 epw checks in 17 min (jobs 56-253 s incl. warm-up); solve 31-83 s per design.

## Memory (record for P1)

Peak VRAM above the pre-solve baseline reached **7,360 MiB at 6,049 dofs** (osse_axi best, epw 8; baseline 341 MiB on
8,188 MiB): 12 kHz verify of these horns is at the edge of the 8 GB card. Per-design solve 31 s (3.5k dofs) to 83 s
(6.1k dofs) for 25 frequencies + 1,024-point balloon. 12 kHz SEARCH or larger horns at 12 kHz need P1 (fused CUDA
Burton-Miller assembly, a solver change, post-beta).
