## 3. The pinned artifact contract (written by `search/cma_me.py`, read by `gui/data.py`)

- `archive.json`: `{"algorithm", "descriptors", "bins", "ranges", "cells", "stats"}`; cells carry
  `{"bin", "center", "objective", "design_hash", "eval_no"}` (occupied only, sorted).
- `archive_heatmap.json`: EXACT top-level `{"bins", "ranges", "descriptors", "cells"}`; per cell
  EXACT `{"h_center", "v_center", "objective", "design_hash"}` — enforced by exact-key assertions in
  `tests/test_search_cma_me.py` against a producer-written file.
- Descriptor measures: NaN-safe in-band means of `MetricSet.bw_h_deg`/`bw_v_deg` over
  `cfg.target.band_lo_hz..band_hi_hz`; ranges pinned to the metrics' own unit `(0,180)` degrees
  (data-independent → archives comparable/resumable; `cma_me.py` :50).
- Budget semantics (both paths): `n_new` counts NON-CACHED evaluations overall; `exhausted()`
  before every candidate; a budget-cut partial batch is never told; resume = deterministic replay
  with cached rows told at zero spend — EXCEPT `solve_failed` rows (never memoised, inherited from
  `cma.py`), which re-solve after a resume (docstring caveat per Oracle).
