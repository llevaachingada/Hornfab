## Ambiguities resolved conservatively

1. **I2 CSV column shift (25 of 55 rows).** Some `one_line` values contain unquoted commas, so
   `csv.DictReader` pushed the tail of `one_line` into `key_conclusion` and the real key conclusion
   into overflow keys. I rejoined the overflow fields before the last one into `one_line` and used
   the last overflow field as `key_conclusion`. Spot-checked against three sources,
   `ATH_PUBLISHED_SEEDS_80x60_2026-11-16.md`, `SEARCH_MATH_DOSSIER_2026-11.md` and
   `typologies/RETURNED_LIP_CD_BACKBONE_RESEARCH_2026-11-25.md`; the reconstructed conclusions
   match the documents. No research conclusion was invented.
2. **Two `unclear` conclusions kept honest.** The inventory records
   `COMPRESSION_DRIVER_LIBRARY_2026-10-28.md` as "unclear (catalogue, no overall recommendation)" and
   `fable_architecture_2026-11-16/README.md` as "unclear (index and reading order)". Both are written
   as catalogue/index-only findings rather than given a conclusion the source does not state.
3. **Missing dates.** `SEARCH_MATH_DOSSIER_2026-11.md` has no CSV date and 30 committed queue images
   have no date; both read `undated` rather than a guessed date. (`docs/queue/README.md` does not
   carry dates.)
4. **Evidence `queue` label.** The I3 `topic_family` label `queue` covers 113 rows: the 112 committed
   files plus the evidence folder `gpu_queue_resolved_2026-11-18/`. The folder is grouped under
   "Campaigns, nights and artifacts"; the 112 files are the final queue section.
5. **Grouping choices.** `12 kHz verify` sits under "Mesh, frequency and termination policy" with
   the 12 kHz screen policy row. `campaigns/` (labelled `BO cohort`) sits with the BO studies rather
   than the campaign nights, because its question is a campaign-arm comparison. `freeform sculpt`
   sits with the sculpted/R-OSSE rows.
6. **Folder links.** Evidence folder rows link to the directory (target ends in `/`, which
   `Test-Path` resolves); the section preamble tells the reader to open the folder's `README.md`.
   This keeps the inventory's `folder` value literal and exactly once.
7. **Non-current rows.** I3 `superseded` and `negative-result` rows stay in place with a
   `**superseded**` or `**negative**` tag (12 rows: 4 superseded, 8 negative) instead of being moved
   or dropped. `BENCH_CPU_VS_CUDA` and `BENCH_AUDIT` are both kept so the retraction reads in place.
8. **Queue inventory coverage.** The I3 inventory catalogues 112 of the 143 top-level files now in
   `docs/queue/` (142 before this README). The 30 uncatalogued files are mostly newer night configs
   (2026-11-19 to 2026-11-25) already named in evidence folders' `queue_files` column. I did not add
   them, to keep the exactly-112 contract; both the catalog queue section and the queue README state
   the scope. The integrator may want to extend I3 and this section together.
