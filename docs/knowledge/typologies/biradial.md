# Biradial

- **Principle:** The Keele constant-directivity lineage uses distinct flare behavior and transitions to shape coverage. “Biradial” has multiple historical usages, so identify the particular design before asserting its construction.
- **Useful objectives:** For a CD brief, examine horizontal and vertical coverage over the specified band, polar smoothness, response, loading, and transition behavior; retain the mounting and source conditions.
- **Limits / wrong assumptions:** A rectangular mouth or two differing radii alone does not prove a Keele construction or constant directivity. Coverage and low-frequency control depend on the complete geometry and aperture. No universal Keele/CD target applies across briefs.
