# Multiple-entry horns (MEH)

- **Principle:** Multiple drivers or frequency bands couple into a shared horn through separate entries. Their geometry, placement and relative phase affect the combined field.
- **Useful objectives:** Assess the complete coupled system: individual and summed responses, polar behavior, crossover integration, entry effects and loading under the intended drive conditions.
- **Limits / wrong assumptions:** A single-source exterior waveguide solve does not validate the multiple-entry system. Entry geometry, phase, crossover and enclosure matter; size does not eliminate low-frequency limits. New MEH work was paused in the documented project context; check current status before acting. No universal Keele/CD target is implied.
