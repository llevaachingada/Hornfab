# Salmon / hyperbolic expansion

- **Principle:** Salmon's horn family describes expansion using a parameter commonly called T; plane-wave and spherical-wavefront constructions are distinct. Azimuth-varying variants can explore non-axisymmetric shapes.
- **Useful objectives:** Evaluate loading and response together with desired radiation, bandwidth, size and termination for the actual construction.
- **Limits / wrong assumptions:** T or a cross-shaped appearance alone does not identify a complete horn. The repository's X-shaped experiments are not a reproduction of AudioHorn's commercial X-Shape; spherical constructions have geometric limits. No universal Keele/CD target is implied.
- **Sources:** [Salmon and X-horn dossier](../../research/typologies/SALMON_X_HORN_EXPANSION_2026-10-25.md), [X-shape research](../../research/typologies/X_SHAPE_AUDIOHORN_RESEARCH_2026-10-25.md).
