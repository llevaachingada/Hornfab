## DSP-aware offline rerank

`tools/analysis/hifi_rescore.py` computes `J_dsp = J_v7 − weighted(flat + flat_cv) + seat/t_seat + 0.5*xo/t_xo + pir/t_pir + 0.5*rough/t_rough`; passive adds `shape/t_shape`. Seat is listening-window frequency-dependent spread after per-angle mean removal, XO is that spread from crossover to 1.6× crossover, PIR is the linear-trend residual of predicted in-room minus listening-window response, rough is the listening-window cubic residual, and shape its linear residual. Tolerances are medians of the v7 top decile within the sampled brief group (minimum three, floor 0.05 dB), not established perceptual thresholds. It retains v7 coverage, **dislope**, and other terms, so cannot fix a mismatched brief; it neither changes nor drives the queued objective.

The response uses unit-velocity conversion and CTA-2034-style approximations, including rear response approximated by ±90° cuts, rather than a complete room or actual equalizer/driver/crossover model. Missing responses and unsupported angle grids are skipped; too few band frequencies yield non-finite terms. Screen-grid ranks need verification and sensitivity checks; calibration depends on the sampled cohort. Separately generated reports recalibrate tolerances, so their DSP numbers are not directly comparable across arms unless a common reference cohort, tolerances, frequency band and screen/verify mode are frozen and applied consistently. On the rejected OSSE v8 screen run `5d1082` (32 designs), v7 winner `5c8864decc06` has J 3.924, DSP 8.291 and moves #1 to #12 (passive #11); seed `7de69ffa75e8` has J 3.992, DSP 6.578 and moves #2 to #3. Seat spread is 1.10 versus 0.59 dB and XO spread 0.48 versus 0.28 dB. This demonstrates ranking sensitivity in one run, not a validated universal listening metric. Full evidence: `docs/evidence/HOME_OSSE_HIFI_RERANK_2026-11-16.md`.
