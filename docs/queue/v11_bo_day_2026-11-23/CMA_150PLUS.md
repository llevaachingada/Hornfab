## Reconciliation before handing a cohort to BO

- Keep the physical brief, bounds, generator/solver/mesh/grid and component schema fixed within each cohort. Keep open and closed tasks separate. For the open transfers, an above-nominal horn is eligible within the sanity envelope. Report actual outer dimensions and apply a product size filter at shortlist time if needed.
- Keep the refusal watchdog. New follow-ups suppress bound-pin stops on their frozen active keys, and their stall/no-gain windows exceed one block. These jobs are collecting candidate coverage as well as improving incumbents. Record refusal rates, pinned parameters, first gain and best-at index by tranche. If a hard failure interrupts a job, inspect the cause before a same-box makeup tranche. Widened bounds require a new identity and separate authorization.
- Reconcile raw attempts, distinct backend calls, successful stored responses, exact screen replay, component parity, projection and duplicate exclusions. Provide a per-run and per-identity table with an explicit 150 verdict. Add only the actual shortfall with a bounded same-box CMA block if safe. A smaller number of rows in an artifact or a solo good J is insufficient.
- Keep multi-seed holdouts, the predeclared Home Fold A confirmation, and verification separate from training selection as appropriate. If reserving additional holdout trajectories would leave fewer than 150 qualified training rows, collect the extra same-identity CMA data. Verify only acoustically credible finalists on the matched verify brief, and preserve scalar vs metric BO tests and worker admission in the [implementation plan](../../research/metric_bo_2026-11-22/11_METRIC_BO_EXECUTION_PLAN.md).
