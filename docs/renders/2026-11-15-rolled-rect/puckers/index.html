All arms optimize depth from 192 to 204 mm, horizontal-profile depth scale from 0.98 to 1.02, and transverse crown from 18 to 22 mm. Seeds are 198 mm, 1.00 and 20 mm. Mouth dimensions, throat, wall thickness, join rounding and pucker location stay fixed.

Each arm receives 48 attempted evaluations, seed 1234, population 8 and normalized initial step 0.15. Initial physical steps are 1.8 mm depth, 0.006 profile scale, 0.6 mm crown; H/V steps are 0.9/1.2 mm for moderate relief and 0.9/0.9 mm for strong relief. Flat geometry penalties count against the budget. Measured random-box refusals were 1/64, 2/64 and 2/64; these are guarded failures, not unvalidated meshes. All tested extreme combinations passed, with at most 4,055 DOFs under the unchanged 6,000 cap.

Relief starts at 30%, peaks at 80% and ends at 96% of the reference vertical-profile arc. It vanishes around the throat and returned edge. The inputs describe wall-centre axial motion, not total ridge-to-diagonal distance: the strong seed's 7/9 mm inputs give 15/17 mm centre-to-diagonal relief at the window peak. Fine bands in the renders come from the screen mesh.

The common brief targets 700 Hz and 80 H x 50 V coverage. Shape feasibility does not demonstrate those acoustic targets. Each winner is queued for denser-frequency verification; spatial mesh convergence is a separate check.
