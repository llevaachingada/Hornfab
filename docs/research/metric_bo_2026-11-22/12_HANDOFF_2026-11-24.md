**Worker + refit.** `studies/metric_refit.py` owns the only refit transaction: validate a fresh
canonical screen (catalog physical ID, six components, dataset reference, projected design,
parity) -> publish immutable training artifact -> publish `metric-refit-snapshot-v1` -> append
`metric_refit_pointer` -> advance. Refit every 4 accepted unique screens; verify/cache/duplicate/
failed/parity-refused rows are excluded; backend calls are counted once. `MetricPipelineStudyEvaluator`
routes `metric_composite_v1` through `MetricTrainingBinding` + `fit_and_propose(require_identity=True)`
and restores the journal/pointer on restart.

**Package + CLI.** `studies/package.py` has a versioned `real_metric_v1` contract with approvals,
caps, lane, deadline, code/dependency pins and 40/64-hex pin rules. `studies/cli.py` has
`metric-package` and `run --dry-run/--execute`; execution additionally requires
`APPROVED_FOR_CANARY`, `owner_authorization.authorized=true`, `HORNLAB_METRIC_REAL_EXECUTE=1` and
the pinned interpreter. Offline/synthetic metric packages still cannot execute.
