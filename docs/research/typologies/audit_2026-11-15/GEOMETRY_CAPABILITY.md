# Geometry capability: bounded source and seed controls

2026-11-15. Read-only CPU geometry evaluation; no mesh generation, solver, GPU or BO calls. Reproduce with `.venv\Scripts\python.exe docs/research/typologies/audit_2026-11-15/geometry_controls.py`. Machine-readable results: [geometry_controls.json](geometry_controls.json); source, YAML SHA-256 and exact inputs are included. The parent process starts one new Python subprocess per seed so each opt-in bridge is installed in isolation. This is a sampled numerical audit, not evidence for full-domain readiness, a watertight body, acoustic correctness or faithful Crowe CAD.

## Independent source-equation checks

An independently typed calculation of Batík's [OS-SE Eq. 5](https://at-horns.eu/release/OS-SE%20Waveguide.pdf), pp. 3–7, was evaluated at 1001 stations against the installed `hornlab_mesher.profile_formulas.profile_points`, once with L=135 mm, r0=18 mm, a0=4.2°, a=41.5°, s=.9, n=3.7, q=.995, k=1, and again with L=120, r0=12.7, a0=7.5°, a=39°, s=.7, n=4, q=.99, k=1.8. They are test parameters, not an exact reproduction of Tritonia (its published script says r0=36 mm). Maximum coordinate errors are in JSON. This explicitly tests degree conversion and nonunit k for these examples, not every profile mode, morph, extension or geometry adapter.

For the complete [R-OSSE rev7](https://at-horns.eu/release/R-OSSE%20Waveguide%20rev7.pdf) p. 7, Fig. 4 example, independently evaluated p. 4 equations at 1001 t samples agree with the installed evaluator to 2.84e-14 mm maximum absolute coordinate error. Parameters: R=130 mm, r0=12.7 mm, a0=7.5°, a=39°, k=1.8, r=.3, b=.3, m=.8, q=3.7. Start is (0,12.7), end is (57.467,130), max x=77.701 mm, supporting the source's 260 mm width and slightly under 80 mm depth. R is radius, not diameter; it is a distinct quantity from r0. The result tests the main curve without a prefix, truncation, angular modulation, morph or solid body. Agreement checks formula transcription and evaluation, not independent mesh fidelity or acoustics.

**Throat-angle nuance requiring attention:** with q>1, positive k,m, no stretch/prefix, the rev7 equations have `x'(0)=L*m/sqrt(r^2+m^2)` and `y'(0)=L*tan(a0)`. Their physical tangent is therefore `atan(tan(a0)*sqrt(r^2+m^2)/m)`, not exactly a0 unless r=0. For the published example, a0=7.5° produces 8.0036° limiting tangent; the 1/1000-step secant is 8.1446°. This is a consequence of the *published* formula and matching implementation, not a degree-conversion error. Exact driver matching requires checking the actual adapter/profile junction, not relabeling a0. This derivation does not extend unqualified to q≤1, zero m/k or stretch/prefix cases.

No stock CLI support is implied for the three bridge-only types. Quarter grids are resampled with at least 33 angular rows for five azimuth cuts; this changes discrete surface sampling from the YAML's exact row count. All start radii sampled at 18.0 mm. First-segment angle is `atan2(delta radius, delta axial)` and **not a limiting tangent**. For steep initial curvature or coarse initial samples its estimate is biased; do not diagnose a throat mismatch from the table alone. The ext-throat source radius is 18 mm even though the main OSSE seed r0=27.0507 mm: the extension tapers backward from the main throat; its source tangent is 4.5° in this seed. The formula-based biradial first segments differ from the nominal 2° driver angle after FREEFORM generation/interpolation; the near-identical 170/171 results do not establish the limiting tangent or locate the cause. Check with smaller independent source-neighborhood steps and exact underlying anchor/interpolator before making a failure claim.

Negative dz interval counts establish only that the sampled **inner meridional curve** turns back under the current coordinate direction. They do not establish Crowe's claimed wrap angle, a thick bead, valid exterior, mesh orientation, junction clearance or topology. Different numbers of intervals are not physical return angles. Curvature here uses finite differences of angle along sampled 2D `(z,r)` arc length; maxima exclude two points at either end, depend on spacing and can sharpen at junctions. They are diagnostics rather than convergence thresholds; a changing maximum is not itself a demonstrated geometric fold.

## Native controls and spiral coupling

Both native rect and section seed pairs were re-resolved after independently zeroing the active relief amplitudes at the *same sample stations*. For radial seeds with 3 mm H and 4 mm V, observed maximum displacements are ~3 and ~4 mm along the respective H/V radial axes, with exactly zero z displacement. For axial seeds with 3/4 mm H/V amplitudes the corresponding maximum z displacements are ~3/4 mm, with exactly zero xy displacement. Peak differences from exact amplitudes (<0.00005 mm) are from sampling the smooth window. This proves coordinate activity and axis separation at these seeds only, not acoustic independence, full domain safety, or equivalence to ATH's original angular profile/morph. See JSON for all component maxima and four intermediate meridians.

The archived ES spiral's `tools/geometry/rolled_rect.py::surface_at` constructs one normalized law `f(t)=uref(t)/u180` and uses `h=r0+f*(W/2-r0)+common` and `v=r0+f*(H/2-r0)+common`; subtracting the same throat blend `common` and dividing by the respective radial spans gives the **same f(t)**. Hence H and V do not have independently selected radial flare laws, even though width, height, normalized azimuth and `depth_h_ratio` change their spatial profiles. This algebra applies to the shared base and not indiscriminately to later signed or radial deformations. It is not a claim of equal coverage or acoustic behavior.

## Cross-section area and sampling limits

The JSON reports quarter-symmetry polygon area projected onto the xy plane at matched **array indices**, after mirroring the quadrant. For an 18 mm circular throat, expected area pi*18²=1017.876 mm²; finite polygons give approximately 1017.5–1017.8 mm². This is a discretization check, not a verification of downstream cross-sectional area. The H/V and diagonal samples often have different z at the same station: e.g. ES spiral mid-ring spans 57.79 mm in z and Crowe-panel last ring 117.73 mm. A projected nonplanar-ring polygon is not a physical area at a single axial plane. A true constant-z section requires intersections with the complete inner surface and, for returns, handling multiple branches and contour topology; it was not calculated here. Similarly, a common-index comparison is not an equal-z comparison.

The normal/dihedral, self-intersection, watertight-body and clearance reports cannot be concluded from these profiles. Full real mesh/solver verification remains outside this run and subject to the current owner hold. Only two source equation cases and the named seeds were checked; no rank, boundary-box or full-domain conclusion is made.
