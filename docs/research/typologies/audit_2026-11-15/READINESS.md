## Confirmed corrections

1. **Source:** FaitalPRO says HF1440 has a35.8 mm combined exit, not a2-degree cone. Radius18 mm/angle2deg are historical interface assumptions. No substitute angle is established; see manufacturer links in [DRIVER_SOURCE](DRIVER_SOURCE.md).
2. **ATH/Crowe:** archived z relief is not radial morph; native inward hybrid is not Tritonia. Tritonia's published r0=36 conflicts with its1.4-inch description. OS-SE and R-OSSE are distinct equations; their q controls differ. Crowe model sections/photos constrain a reconstruction without specifying exact intermediate surfaces or proprietary equations.
3. **Returns:** negative dz exists in sampled spiral, section, R-OSSE and OSSE-lip meridians. A blanket “no true return” is inaccurate. A returned curve does not establish a thick exterior bead, smooth junction or valid closed body.
4. **Throat:** the spiral kernel includes a2-degree source term; “leaves the throat axially” is not an exact tangent statement. R-OSSE's physical limiting tangent generally differs from its nominal a0 even when the implementation exactly matches the paper. Large first-segment secants alone cannot diagnose a source mismatch.
5. **Objective:** stored DI and original scores replay exactly for32 seed/winner responses. Slope uses unweighted regression against log10(f), in dB/decade; dB/oct=slope*log10(2). `distd=std(DI)` penalizes a perfectly smooth rise; the smooth brief's comments incorrectly describe it as only irregularity. Its5dB/dec target lacks a universal preference basis. Keep both legacy briefs as historical experiments; do not promote the smooth brief as a proven correction.
6. **Absolute DI and diagonal:**80x50 Gaussian target is13.079574dB in current v7, not the dossier's~10.1. Diagonal target is65deg; same80x50 axis crossings permit different diagonal contours (illustrative59.92..66.81deg). This demonstrates a shape prior, not a proof every diagonal penalty is unfair. H/V use outermost crossings, diagonal uses first crossings; investigate lobes separately. Current `flat` is detrended residual roughness, not raw on-axis SPL standard deviation.
7. **Source normalization:** saved responses use unit normal **acceleration**, at10m, full-balloon DI with sin(theta) weighting; not a measured HF1440 velocity field. Source phase/curvature, mounting and complete woofer/crossover system remain unestablished. f11 is an ideal duct diagnostic, not a universal sharp validity cutoff.
8. **Fold:** original Crowe seed/winner raw adjacent-normal angles176.463/153.214deg reproduce. All seven audited originals have zero same-directed shared edges, index-nonmanifold edges, degenerate or duplicate-index faces; boundaries lie on symmetry planes. “Inverted triangles” was not proven. Acute junction, intersections and clearance still need explicit role-aware tests; see [witness](mesh_witness_crowe_seed.png).
9. **Identity:** native rolled family covers inner+outer in its materialized hash. Generic grid BO routes are rejected; other supported formulae cannot be assumed to have equivalent exterior dedup. Bridge source hashes include CLI bytes, so a convenience edit to a wrapper can invalidate old recipes. Original identities were preserved.
