# Bass box, 30-120 Hz (M10) — designs, recommendation, build sheet

2026-10-31. Work package M10 (owns `tools/bassbox.py`, seven new `examples/drivers/*.yaml`,
`tests/test_bassbox.py`, this file). CPU only: numpy + scipy; no GPU, no Julia, no solver, no mesher.
Datasheet values were transcribed from the makers' own pages/PDFs on 2026-10-31 (URLs in the driver
files and in section 1); nothing here is invented.

## Verdict

**One FaitalPRO 18XL1800 in a 54.9 L net (61.8 L gross) sealed box, Linkwitz-transformed from its
64.6 Hz / Q 0.89 in-box resonance to 26 Hz / Q 0.707, plus a 15 Hz LR4 infrasonic high-pass and five
PEQ bands, is the recommended single box.** It reaches **116.4 dB at 40 Hz** and **123.1-126.6 dB
from 63 to 120 Hz** (sine, 1 m, half space, before room gain), i.e. **+4.4 dB / +5.1 dB** over the
owner targets, and is flat within **0.44 dB** from 30 to 120 Hz after DSP. Max DSP boost is
**+12.2 dB** (the Linkwitz transform at the bottom octave); the driver is at 1600 W AES / 92.5 V rms
at the limit. Cabinet is **516 x 516 x 304 mm** external (18 mm walls; 480 x 480 x 268 mm internal).

The stereo pair is **2 x the same box**: +6 dB coherent in half space below ~120 Hz, i.e. **122.4 dB
at 40 Hz, 129.1-132.6 dB at 63-120 Hz**; the pair is the build that "keeps up with a powered 12 in +
horn PA" with real reserve. 124 L gross total.

The **smallest** box that meets the targets is a **FaitalPRO 15XL1400 in 38.0 L net (42.6 L gross)**,
449 x 449 x 286 mm external: 113.3 dB at 40 Hz (Xmax-limited) and 119.3 dB at 63 Hz (P_AES-limited),
+1.3 dB at both — it meets, but with no reserve and only +0.6 dB at 120 Hz if the bass channel is
low-passed LR4 at 120 Hz. It is listed as the compact alternative, not the recommendation.

A vented box is not recommended for this target with these drivers: every shortlisted driver needs
68-81 L net to hit 40 Hz through the 17 m/s port limit, which is larger than the sealed 18XL1800 and
gives no more 40 Hz output (114-115 dB, port-limited) than the sealed box's 116.4 dB (power-limited).

## 1. Targets and criterion

Owner targets (C0, 2026-10-30; M10 task): flat 30-120 Hz with DSP; **>= 112 dB at 40 Hz** and
**>= 118 dB at 63-120 Hz**, sine, 1 m, half space, **before** room/corner gain; as small as meets it;
one or two boxes. Crossover to the MEH at the brief's `hp_hz` (120 Hz T2 / 160 Hz T1).

The criterion used by the tool:

* **Flatness**: the DSP'd on-axis response is within +/-2 dB (RMS <= 1 dB) over 30-120 Hz, with the
  PEQ boost capped at +6 dB (the LT is a targeting filter, counted separately); the -3 dB point of
  the DSP'd response must be at or below 30 Hz.
* **Headroom**: `spl_max(f) >= 112` at 40 Hz and `>= 118` everywhere in 63-120 Hz, where `spl_max` is
  the smaller of the three C11 ceilings (cone excursion = Xmax, voice-coil `V^2/Re = P_AES`, port
  peak particle velocity = 17 m/s). This is a **per-frequency ceiling**, not a flat-programme level:
  a flat programme is limited by its weakest octave (the 30-40 Hz Xmax/port limit), and that number
  (`flat-programme max`) is reported separately for honesty.
* **Size**: the smallest net volume that passes wins; ties go to the largest minimum margin, then the
  smallest PEQ boost.

## 3. Model (`tools/bassbox.py`)

Lumped, `exp(+j omega t)`, SI rms, solver-free. The port branch, `L_eff` and `Q_port` are the C7
model of `hornlab_search/meh/network.py`; the box, DSP and limits are this tool.

* Box compliance `C_ab = V_b/(rho c^2)`, leakage `R_al = Ql/(omega C_ab)` with **Ql = 7**; port
  impedance `Z_port = x/Q_port + j x`, `x = omega rho L_eff / S_port`, **Q_port = 10**,
  `L_eff = l_neck + n_end*0.85*r_eq`, **n_end = 2** for a free-standing box (both mouths radiate).
  The acoustic load is the compliance in parallel with the leak and the port.
* Driver: `Ze = Re + j omega Le`,
  `Zm = Rms + j omega Mms + 1/(j omega Cms) + Bl^2/Ze + Sd^2 Z_box`,
  cone velocity `v = Bl e/(Ze Zm)`, `U_d = Sd v`, `Z_in = 1/i`.
  Front-side radiation resistance is not added to the mechanical load (standard T/S; it is ~1 % of
  Rms here and the published `Mms` already carries the air mass).
* SPL: half-space monopole sum of cone and port at 1 m,
  `p = j omega rho (U_d + U_p)/(2 pi r)`; the cone and port are coherent (same baffle), hence the
  cancellation below tuning and the 4th-order rolloff.
* DSP: `dsp.lr(f, 15 Hz, 4, "hp")` (infrasonic LR4), the Linkwitz transform
  `(s^2 + (w0/Q0)s + w0^2)/(s^2 + (wc/Qc)s + wc^2)` from the box's **model** `(f0, Q0)` to 26 Hz /
  0.707, then five `dsp.peaking` bands at 30/38/50/65/90 Hz with Q 0.9/1.2/1.2/1.2/1.0. Gains are a
  bounded least-squares fit to the measured box response over 30-120 Hz (boost <= +6 dB, cut
  <= -12 dB); centres and Qs are fixed, as in a real DSP preset.
* Limits (C11): excursion = `sqrt(2)|v|/omega <= Xmax` (one-way peak), `|e|^2/Re <= P_AES`, port
  `sqrt(2)|U_port|/S_port <= 17 m/s`. `spl_max(f) = SPL(1 V) + 20 log10 e_lim(f)`, with `e_lim` the
  smallest of the three voltage ceilings; `limiting` names the binding one.
  A flat programme can reach `min_f spl_max(f)` over its band (EQ changes the relative level, not
  the driver ceiling).
* Energy: `P_electrical = P_joule + P_mechanical + P_port + P_leak` exactly; the radiated power is
  reported separately (`rho omega^2 |U_d+U_p|^2/(4 pi c)`), as the model has no radiation back-load.

Cabinet geometry (for the build sheet): front-mounted driver on a baffle whose internal width and
height are `frame_OD + 20 mm` (driver clearance), depth from the gross volume
`V_net + port + 0.25 Sd x depth` (driver displacement estimate), 18 mm walls. Vented designs use a
floor slot port spanning the internal width minus 50 mm, with the neck length from the tuning
formula; a candidate is rejected if the neck does not fit the internal depth minus 60 mm.

**2 x the same box**, placed within 1 m of each other (coherent coupling below ~120 Hz):

### 5.3 Compact alternative (the smallest that meets)

**FaitalPRO 15XL1400, sealed 38.0 L net / 42.6 L gross**, 449 x 449 x 286 mm external (internal

### 5.4 Why not vented?

The vented designs are 68-81 L net, i.e. 12-27 L larger than the sealed 18XL1800, and their 40 Hz
output is *lower* (113.9-115.3 dB) because the 17 m/s port limit binds before Xmax. They are 0-2 dB
louder from 63 to 120 Hz, but with a larger box, a 25-42 mm slot port (chuffing risk at high level),
and a 4-7 dB cut/hump DSP curve. The sealed box is smaller, has no port, and has 4.4/5.1 dB of
per-frequency reserve; that is the recommendation.

## 6. Validation

* `pytest tests/test_bassbox.py -q` -> **65 passed** (CPU, ~40 s including the design search).
* Full offline suite: see the commit / report; the tool adds no GPU, Julia or network dependency.
* **Textbook B4**: a lossless vented box with `fs = fb`, `Qts = 0.383` and the B4 box constant
  `alpha = Vas/Vb = sqrt(2)` is **-3.01 dB at fs** (asserted within 0.2 dB), passband 0.0 dB at
  2 fs. The task text says "alpha 1"; in this (standard) lossless model `alpha = 1` gives **0.0 dB at
  fs** (a flat-passband alignment, not Butterworth). The test file asserts both numbers and records
  the arithmetic: the `s^2` coefficient must be `2 + sqrt(2)`, which at `h = 1` needs
  `alpha = sqrt(2)`; `alpha = 1` gives 3. The 0.383 value is the rounded `1/(2 sqrt 2)`.
* **Energy balance**: `P_electrical = P_joule + P_mechanical + P_port + P_leak` to < 1e-9 for a
  sealed and a vented box over 20-200 Hz (tested), and the radiated-power bookkeeping matches the
  far-field monopole relation.
* **Port velocity**: `sqrt(2)|U_rms|/S_port` against a hand value (0.012 m^3/s, 0.018 m^2 ->
  0.9428 m/s) and against the box's own port flow; the port tuning `c/(2 pi) sqrt(S/(V L_eff))`
  inverts the length formula exactly.
* **Limits**: at `e_lim` the binding quantity (Xmax, `e^2/Re` or 17 m/s) is exactly at its limit and
  none of the three is exceeded, for every point of a 25-150 Hz sweep.
* **Driver files**: all seven parse against C8, carry the `derived:` list with the exact formula text
  in `notes`, the derived `Mmd` matches `mmd_from_mms` to 0.02 g, the derived `Cms` matches the Vas
  formula to 1e-5 relative, and the model's Fs is within 8 % of the published Fs (the two
  inconsistent datasheets are named in the file notes and in section 2).

## 7. Caveats

* **Half space, 1 m, coherent monopole.** The model has no baffle step, no room, no floor reflection
  and no directivity; the owner's targets are also half-space before room gain, so the comparison is
  apples to apples, but a real room/corner will add bass.
* **Xmax basis.** B&C's "maximum linear excursion" and FaitalPRO's figure are treated as one-way
  peak; 18 Sound prints "peak"; Beyma's PDF contradicts itself (table 8 mm, bullet +/-9 mm) and the
  table value 8 mm is used (recorded in the file). If a maker's number is peak-to-peak, the
  excursion ceilings here are 6 dB optimistic.
* **Datasheet inconsistencies.** The derived C8 pair (Cms from Vas, Mmd from Mms) gives the model Fs
  in section 2. The designs use the model's own f0/Q0 for the LT, so they are self-consistent, but a
  real 15TBX100 will not quite match the 15TBX100 row (7.8 % Fs error in the datasheet).
* **The LT boost.** A 12-15 dB boost at 30 Hz is normal for a small sealed box, but it uses amplifier
  voltage and driver power: the P_AES limit in the table already includes it. Do not add a second
  "house curve" boost below 40 Hz without re-checking Xmax.
* **Box geometry.** Internal dimensions assume 18 mm walls, a front-mounted driver and the stated
  gross volume; bracing, a gasket and the driver's own displacement change the net volume by a few
  percent. Keep the net air volume within +/-5 % of the table (stuffing is a small effective-volume
  increase, so line rather than fill).
* **Port.** The vented designs use a floor slot with n_end = 2 and Q_port = 10; a real slot needs a
  roundover and a 1:1 aspect check. The reported 17 m/s is a peak particle velocity (C11); if the
  build chuffs, the port must be larger, which lengthens the neck and may not fit.
* **Not in this model**: passive radiators, driver inductance beyond `j omega Le`, cone break-up,
  thermal power compression, and the MEH's own LF load. The bass box is a separate box by C0.
