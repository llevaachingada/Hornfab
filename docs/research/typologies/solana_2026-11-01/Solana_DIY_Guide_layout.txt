Design Overview
This is a compact 2-way top that prioritizes smooth, well controlled directivity, portability, and moderate output in a symmetric
package.




                                  Genetic Algorithm Shape Optimization
                                  The Solana platform represents a case study in algorithmic loudspeaker shape optimization using the
                                  boundary element method. The waveguide profile was iterated and developed via an evolutionary
                                  algorithm to minimize off-axis lobing and polar irregularities, while achieving pattern control down to the
                                           low-midrange. Version 1.0 was the result of a focused optimization campaign with a basic
                                           genetic algorithm script that produced 350 candidates over 7 generations. Version 2.0 is the
                                           product of a more advanced GA that simulated over 4,400 unique shapes before arriving at the
                                           final geometry.




Enclosure Design
The provided enclosure plans specify a simple ~25 liter rear chamber tuned at
70Hz with a single rear port that serves as a carrying handle. The quad 6.5”
woofers are effectively loaded into a 6th order bandpass system that is best
suited for crossing over to subs around 100Hz using a 4th order acoustic slope.
However the enclosure can be modified by lengthening the port for full-range
use at lower SPLs. A target rear chamber tuning frequency of 50Hz is
recommended for low-tuned enclosure modifications.

          Minimum print volume: 250x210x220mm for 4-piece assembly OR 340x320x340mm for 2-piece print.

DSP Settings
Important note - for designs such as the Solana, it's critical to state that the electrical crossover points and slopes (the ones
configured in DSP) are not always equal to the acoustic crossover points and slopes (what the drivers are actually
producing). For best results, configure target acoustic slopes within your measurement software, and then use a combination
of gain, parametric EQs, and electrical LPFs/HPFs to align the measured response until it matches the targets.

The following DSP settings are provided as target curves for tuning. The Loudspeaker Measurement & System Alignment
Guide is a comprehensive resource that can be referenced for measuring and aligning the Solana.

Channel 1 (Woofers)
Target Acoustic LPF: 1200Hz LR 36dB/octave
Target Acoustic HPF: 100Hz LR 24dB/octave
Minimum Safe Electrical HPF: 70Hz LR 12dB/octave

Channel 2 (Compression Driver)
Target Acoustic LPF: None
Target Acoustic HPF: 1200Hz LR 36dB/octave
Minimum Safe Electrical HPF: 1000Hz LR 12dB/octave




Measure with a 10 degree microphone reference axis.
