Where things are at

The pipeline is fast and provenanced: the speed program finished (E0–E9) with a 400/400-eval production night at the new solver pin. The open science questions are about the search, not the solver: too much budget is lost to invalid geometry, one FREEFORM family is pressed against two bounds, the screen score is optimistic, and seed repeatability is still unmeasured. A CPU-lane night (ath_core 14″, seed 7890) is running today.

    Quality-diversity · CMA-ME

    MAP-Elites archives

    The OSSE runs use CMA-ME: CMA-ES emitters filling an 18 × 18 grid of mean horizontal × vertical beamwidth (0–180°, 10° cells). Each filled cell keeps its best design. The archive shows what coverage shapes a family can reach, not just the single best.

    Objective balance

    What the score is made of

    Share of J per term, and whether the best 10% of designs reach each term's tolerance (1× = on target). A large term that is also over tolerance is the bottleneck.

    Search box

    Where the best designs live

    Each track is one knob's search range. The filled band is the proposed range (best-10% span + 5%). Red edges are walls the optimiser pushed against.

    Sensitivity

    Which knobs move the score

    Permutation importance from a random-forest surrogate (ΔJ when the knob is shuffled), plus Spearman ρ for direction. Descriptive, not causal.

    atlas__core_law · OSSE · importance

    Surrogate R² 0.49, so treat the ranking as rough. 25 knobs showed no measurable effect and are candidates to freeze.

    L_mm against J

    Horn length is the dominant driver (ρ −0.70). The best 10% average 309 mm against 218 mm overall.

    lecleach_rollback · FREEFORM · importance

    Surrogate R² 0.97. g_fc_hz is 33× more important than the next knob.

    Knob link candidate

    Among the best 57 atlas__core_law designs, az_a_sf_amp ≈ −0.613 · a_deg + 24.12 (ρ −0.65, R² 0.43). Below the 0.8 bar, so it is a hint, not a rule for merging two knobs.

    Fidelity

    Can we trust a screen score?

    atlas__core_law, 12 designs solved at screen and verify fidelity. On the dashed line both agree.

    All 12 sit above the line: every design scored worse once verified.
    +29.2%median verify vs screen bias
    0.66Spearman ρ, all 12 pairs
    0.50ρ among the screen's top 5
    12 / 567screened designs that were verified

    The bias alone is harmless if ranking holds. It doesn't hold well at the top, so the recommendation is to send the top five or more to verify and choose the winner on verify J.
